How far is the metro? Walking access to STM stations in Montréal

Many students and residents in Montreal rely on the metro every day, but a station that looks close on a map can be a long or difficult walk. This map helps them see which areas have good walking access to an STM metro station and which do not.

This map is for

Students and residents in Montreal who want to check how easy it is to walk to the metro.

What you can do here

Click the box to see only the metro stations you can use with a wheelchair, and click any station to see its line and access. Use the search boxes to find the closest metro station to a grocery store, or the closest open grocery store to a metro station. Choose a station from the dropdown to see every open grocery store within 500 m of it.

    Not in this iteration

    • Real walk times on the street network: still working on walking routes from the Google API.
    • Walking barriers: highways and other barriers are not measured yet. Search distances and the 500 m circles are straight lines, not walks.
    • A stricter grocery list: the city's grocery category also includes kiosks and pharmacies, and the data stops at the Island of Montréal, so stations in Laval and Longueuil may have a closer store than the one shown.
    • One borough focus: I kept the whole metro network to keep the pipeline simple; next I will focus on Côte-des-Neiges–NDG, where many students live and the Décarie expressway creates walking barriers.
    • Transfer stations: stations like Berri-UQAM and Lionel-Groulx appear under one line only, because that is how the STM file stores them.
